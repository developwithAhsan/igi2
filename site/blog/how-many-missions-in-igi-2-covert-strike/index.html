How Many Missions Are in IGI 2: Covert Strike? The Full Campaign Explained

IGI 2 has 19 missions. Here is how the campaign is split, what you do in each stage and what to expect along the way.
MISSIONS19 Missions

By Ahsan · November 21, 2026 · 3 min read

If you are wondering how many missions IGI 2: Covert Strike has, the short answer is 19. The longer answer is more interesting, because those 19 missions take you from frozen mountain ranges to a Libyan prison and, eventually, a guarded rocket launch site.

IGI 2 is built around objectives rather than straight corridors. Each mission hands you a briefing with a list of goals, and most of them can be finished in more than one way. Some are best played loud, with a rifle and a lot of ammunition. Others punish you the moment an alarm goes off, so patience and a silenced weapon matter far more.

Where the campaign begins

You start as David Jones, a former British SAS operator working for the Institute for Geotactical Intelligence. The opening missions, including Mission 1: Infiltration and Mission 2: Deep in the Mines, send you into the Carpathian mountains to recover EMP chips stolen from a US facility. These early levels teach the rhythm of the game: scout first, move quietly, then strike.

The Libya stretch

The middle of the game moves to Libya and is easy to remember because the missions run in a neat chain: Libyan Rendezvous (Mission 8), Prison Escape (Mission 9), Priboi’s Villa (Mission 10), The Airfield (Mission 11), Zaleb’s Stronghold (Mission 12) and Showdown at the Docks (Mission 13). The goal is to find and rescue the arms dealer Priboi, who holds the information you need.

The final stage

From the docks, the story pushes on towards the Spratly Islands and a heavily guarded launch pad, where Jones has to stop a rocket launch before it causes an international incident. The last missions are the tensest in the game, so save your best equipment for them.

Tips for working through all 19

Read every briefing carefully, because objectives change from mission to mission.

Use the Map Computer (M) before you move, not after you are spotted.

Treat stealth levels as puzzles, and combat levels as tests of positioning.

Keep a silenced weapon for the missions that fail on alarm.
