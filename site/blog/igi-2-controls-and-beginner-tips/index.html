IGI 2 Controls and Beginner Tips: Move, Aim and Stay Undetected

Learn the default IGI 2 PC controls and the habits that keep new players alive and unseen.

By Ahsan · November 21, 2026 · 3 min read

Anyone who has ever started IGI 2 knows the first few minutes can feel unforgiving. The good news is that the controls are straightforward, and a handful of habits will carry you through most missions.

The keys you will use constantly

W, A, S, D to move, with the mouse to aim and the left mouse button to fire.

Space to jump and Shift to crouch.

R to reload and F / V to switch weapons.

E to interact with doors, computers and objects.

The tools beginners ignore

Three items change how you play. The Map Computer (M) shows your objectives and the layout around you. Binoculars (B), with zoom on H and N, let you count guards before you commit. Thermal goggles (T) help you spot enemies in the dark.

Five habits that keep you alive

Scout first. Spend ten seconds with the binoculars before moving into a new area.

Crouch near guards, because standing makes you easier to spot.

Choose the right weapon: silenced when stealth matters, noisy only when it does not.

Know the quick load key (F7), so one mistake does not cost you the whole mission.

Do not rush objectives you have not read in the briefing.
