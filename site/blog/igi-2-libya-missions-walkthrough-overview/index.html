IGI 2 Libya Missions Explained: Libyan Rendezvous to Showdown at the Docks

An overview of the IGI 2 Libya missions, from Libyan Rendezvous to Showdown at the Docks, with objectives and tips.

By Ahsan · November 21, 2026 · 3 min read

The Libya stretch is the heart of IGI 2’s campaign. Jones is sent to find the arms dealer Priboi, who is held by Libyan intelligence, and the six missions that follow are among the most memorable in the game.

Mission 8: Libyan Rendezvous

Objectives include meeting a bribed local militiaman who guides you to a surveillance room, shutting down the hotel cameras, collecting an explosive charge, blowing up a refuelling vehicle in the marketplace, neutralising the operatives guarding Priboi and rescuing him from the hotel.

Mission 9: Prison Escape

Things go wrong and Jones ends up in a cell. The goal is to break out and take Priboi with you, then escape the prison.

Mission 10: Priboi’s Villa

Priboi’s villa is full of guards loyal to Major Zaleb Said. Your task is to search it and take a backup of Priboi’s computer records.

Mission 11: The Airfield

A desert airfield where you get your helicopter armed and fuelled. It is a good test of stealth and patience.

Mission 12: Zaleb’s Stronghold

You recover Priboi’s briefcase and deal with Zaleb himself. Expect heavier resistance than in earlier missions.

Mission 13: Showdown at the Docks

At the docks Jones closes in on the traitor behind the whole plot. It sets up the final stretch of the story.

Tips for the whole stretch

Use binoculars on every new area before you move.

Save explosives and grenades for objectives that need them.

Check the Map Computer (M) before each mission starts.
