IGI 2 Story Explained: David Jones, Wu Xing and the Stolen EMP Chips

A spoiler-light walkthrough of the plot of IGI 2: Covert Strike, from the Carpathian mountains to the final launch.

By Ahsan · November 21, 2026 · 3 min read

Light spoilers ahead, though we keep the biggest surprises vague.

The story of IGI 2: Covert Strike is a classic techno-thriller. It starts small, with a theft, and ends with a global threat.

The setup

David Jones, an agent for the Institute for Geotactical Intelligence and a former SAS operator, is sent to the Carpathian mountains in Ukraine. A group of Russian mafia has stolen a set of EMP chips from a high-tech US facility, and Jones has to get them back. His mission director at the start is Phillip White.

A change of handler

Things shift when Jones begins to suspect that the trail leads back to people he trusted. Anya takes over as his mission director, and sends him to Libya to find Priboi, an arms dealer who sold advanced military equipment and who knows who bought it. Priboi is held by Libyan intelligence, and Major Zaleb Said is not a man who gives anything up easily.

From Libya to the launch pad

Priboi’s information points to a deal made at a seaport in Egypt, and from there Jones travels towards the Spratly Islands near China. There, the pieces come together: a rogue Chinese general, Wu Xing, has been arranging events so that EMP weaponry ends up on a space rocket he controls.

The ending

Jones makes his way to the guarded launch site, with Anya and CIA teams close behind, and has to stop the launch before it triggers an international crisis. Wu Xing is the final obstacle, and by the end of the game the threat is stopped.

Why the story works

IGI 2 never asks you to follow a complicated plot. It gives you a clear chain of objectives, a few memorable villains and a sense that every level moves the mission forward. That simplicity is a big part of why people still remember it.
