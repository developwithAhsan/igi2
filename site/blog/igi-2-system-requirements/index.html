IGI 2 System Requirements: Minimum vs Recommended Specs for PC

The official minimum and recommended PC requirements for IGI 2: Covert Strike, plus what they mean on a modern computer.

By Ahsan · November 21, 2026 · 3 min read

IGI 2: Covert Strike came out in 2003, so its hardware demands are small by today’s standards. Here is what the game asks for and how that translates to a modern PC.

Minimum requirements

Operating system: Windows XP or Vista

Processor: 1 GHz

Memory: 128 MB of RAM

Graphics: DirectX 7 compatible 3D graphics card

Input: keyboard and mouse

Recommended requirements

Processor: 1.4 GHz

Memory: 512 MB of RAM

Graphics: DirectX 9 compatible graphics card

What this means today

Any computer bought in the last decade is far beyond these numbers, so performance is rarely the problem. The challenge is software compatibility, because the game was built for the Windows XP era. If the game will not launch, read our guide to running IGI 2 on Windows 10 and 11.

Quick checklist before you install

Confirm your graphics driver is up to date.

Start at a low resolution and raise it later.

Use a legitimate copy of the game from a licensed seller.

Play it on IGI2.online

Open the classic IGI 2 menu, read the controls guide and follow progress on the website. Browser gameplay is being developed with WebAssembly.
