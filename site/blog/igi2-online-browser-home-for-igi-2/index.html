IGI2.online: A Browser Home for IGI 2: Covert Strike Fans

Meet IGI2.online: the classic IGI 2 menu, music, controls guide, FAQ and blog in one place, with in-browser play on the way.

By Ahsan · November 21, 2026 · 3 min read

IGI2.online started with a simple idea: IGI 2: Covert Strike deserves a proper home on the web, built by someone who loves it.

What you can do here today

Open the classic main menu with original-style music and sound effects.

Use the Configuration screen for music, sound effects and fullscreen.

Learn every default key in the controls guide, laid out like a real keyboard.

Read mission overviews, requirements and tips on the blog.

Join the discussion in the comments.

Where we are heading

The goal is a place where you can play IGI 2 in your browser with no download, using WebAssembly. That takes careful engineering, so we ship in steps and share progress on this blog. Read how it works in our guide to playing IGI 2 in your browser.

Why trust this site

Everything here is written by one person, Ahsan, a web developer and digital creator. Facts are checked against reliable sources, the site is clearly labelled as an unofficial fan project, and we do not host pirated games.
