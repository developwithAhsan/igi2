Is IGI 2 Multiplayer? What the Game Offers and What to Expect Today

Does IGI 2: Covert Strike have multiplayer? Yes. Here is how the mode works and what to expect on a modern PC.

By Ahsan · November 21, 2026 · 3 min read

Short answer: yes. IGI 2: Covert Strike is best known for its single-player campaign, but the original PC release also ships with a multiplayer mode, and it is one of the questions fans ask most often.

How multiplayer works

The multiplayer mode puts two opposing teams against each other on dedicated multiplayer maps, with each side working towards objectives rather than simply racing for kills. It keeps the tension of the campaign: weapons feel weighty, a single shot can matter, and a careless step into the open gets punished.

How it differs from the campaign

Human opponents behave far less predictably than campaign guards.

Teamwork and communication matter more than raw aim.

Maps are built around objectives, so positioning beats running and gunning.

Playing it today

IGI 2 launched in 2003, when many multiplayer games relied on online services that have since been retired. Setting up a match today can take some effort, such as a local network or community-run tools, and details vary by version of the game. Check community resources for your copy before you plan a session.

Tips if you are new

Learn the single-player basics first with our controls and beginner tips.

Practise with binoculars and the map computer so you know each layout.

Move deliberately, because noise gives away your position.
