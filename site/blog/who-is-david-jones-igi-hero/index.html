Who Is David Jones? Profile of the Hero of the IGI Series

A profile of David Jones, the former SAS operative at the heart of Project I.G.I. and IGI 2: Covert Strike.

By Ahsan · November 21, 2026 · 3 min read

David Jones is the quiet professional at the centre of the I.G.I. games. He rarely speaks more than he has to, and he is judged entirely by how well he completes the job in front of him.

Background

Jones is a former British SAS operative who works as an agent for the Institute for Geotactical Intelligence (I.G.I.). In the first game he is sent to Tallinn, Estonia, to extract Josef Priboi. In IGI 2 his missions take him from the Carpathian mountains in Ukraine to Libya, an Egyptian seaport and the Spratly Islands.

The people around him

Anya: his mission director and guide in both games.

Phillip White: his mission director at the start of IGI 2.

Priboi: the arms dealer whose information drives the plot.

How he plays

Jones is a stealth-first operator. The gameplay screenshots on this site show his usual kit: an MP5A3, a silenced G-17 pistol, a knife and grenades. The silenced pistol and the knife are for quiet work, while rifles and grenades are for when stealth fails.

Why he lasts

Jones does not have a flashy personality. What stays with players is the feeling that he is an ordinary professional doing extraordinary work, and that every mission is won by planning. Read the full plot in our story explainer.
