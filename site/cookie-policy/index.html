Cookie Policy

How IGI2.online uses cookies and similar technologies

Last updated: November 21, 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies, such as local storage, serve the same purpose. They help sites work properly, remember your choices and understand how the site is used.

How we use them

Essential and preference: remember settings such as your music and sound choices.

Analytics: help us see which pages are popular so we can improve them.

Advertising: third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to show ads based on your previous visits. You can opt out at Google Ads Settings.

Third-party content: services such as comment platforms and web fonts may set their own cookies.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some features may not work properly if you do. For guidance, visit aboutcookies.org.

Updates and contact

We may update this policy as our site changes. See our Privacy Policy for more, or email help@igi2.online.
