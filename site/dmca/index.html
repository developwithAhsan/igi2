DMCA & Copyright Policy

How to report copyright infringement on IGI2.online

Last updated: November 21, 2026

IGI2.online respects the intellectual property rights of others and expects the same from its visitors. If you believe material on this site infringes your copyright, please send us a notice and we will review it promptly.

What to include in your notice

Your full name, address, phone number and email

A description of the copyrighted work you say has been infringed

The exact address (URL) of the material you want removed

A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorised by the owner, the law or an agent

A statement, under penalty of perjury, that your notice is accurate and that you are the owner or authorised to act for the owner

Your physical or electronic signature

Send notices to help@igi2.online with the subject “DMCA Notice”.

Counter-notification

If you believe material was removed by mistake, you may send a counter-notice to the same address with your contact details, a description of the removed material and its former location, and a statement under penalty of perjury that you believe the removal was a mistake.

Repeat infringers

We may disable access for users who repeatedly infringe copyright. Knowingly submitting a false notice may lead to legal liability.
