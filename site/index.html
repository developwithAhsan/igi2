About IGI 2

Project I.G.I.-2: Covert Strike

IGI 2: Covert Strike (also known as Project I.G.I.-2) is a classic tactical first-person shooter developed by Innerloop Studios and published by Codemasters in 2003. You play David Jones, a former British SAS operative working for the Institute for Geotactical Intelligence, across 19 stealth-focused missions where planning, silenced weapons and the Map Computer matter as much as firepower.

IGI2.online is a fan-made tribute for everyone who wants to play IGI 2 online, relive the iconic main menu and music, and learn every IGI 2 control. Explore the controls and FAQ, and share your favourite IGI 2 missions in the comments.

IGI 2 FAQ

Frequently asked questions about IGI 2: Covert Strike

What is IGI 2: Covert Strike?

IGI 2: Covert Strike, also known as Project I.G.I.-2, is a tactical first-person stealth shooter developed by Innerloop Studios and published by Codemasters in 2003. It is the sequel to Project I.G.I.: I’m Going In (2000) and follows ex-SAS operative David Jones through a series of covert missions.

Is this the original IGI 2?

IGI 2: Covert Strike is a genuine classic from 2003, and IGI2.online is a fan-made tribute to it, with the original-style main menu, menu music and a complete controls guide. This is an unofficial project and is not affiliated with Codemasters or Innerloop Studios. All game titles and trademarks belong to their respective owners.

Can I play IGI 2 online in my browser?

IGI2.online is being built around WebAssembly (WASM), the technology that lets demanding, native-style games run inside a modern browser without plugins. The menu you see here already runs in your browser, and in-browser gameplay is being added step by step, so keep checking back.

How many missions does IGI 2 have?

The single-player campaign has 19 missions. Across them David Jones infiltrates guarded compounds, rescues allies, steals data and sabotages enemy operations, using stealth, silenced weapons, binoculars and the Map Computer.

What are the IGI 2 controls?

On PC you move with W, A, S and D, aim with the mouse, fire with the left mouse button, jump with Space and reload with R. The full list of default keys is in the Controls section above, and every key can be changed in the game’s Configuration menu.

What are the IGI 2 system requirements?

The original PC game is light: Windows XP or Vista, a 1 GHz processor (1.4 GHz recommended), 128 MB of RAM (512 MB recommended) and a DirectX 7 compatible 3D graphics card (DirectX 9 recommended), plus a keyboard and mouse.

Does IGI 2 have multiplayer?

Yes. The original game includes a multiplayer mode where two opposing teams compete to complete objectives on special multiplayer maps.

Who is David Jones in IGI 2?

David Jones is the main character, a former British SAS operative who works for the Institute for Geotactical Intelligence (I.G.I.). He relies on stealth, careful planning and precise shooting to complete every mission.

Is IGI2.online free to use?

Yes, browsing IGI2.online, the menu, the controls guide and the community comments is completely free.
