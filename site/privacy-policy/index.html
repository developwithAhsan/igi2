Privacy Policy

How IGI2.online collects, uses and protects your information

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information IGI2.online (“we”, “us”) collects when you visit our website, how we use it and the choices you have. By using the site you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

Log and device data: IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, referring page and the date and time of your visit.

Cookies and similar technologies: small files or local storage used to remember settings and to measure and improve the site.

Information you send us: your name, email address and message if you contact us or post a comment.

How we use information

To run, secure and improve the website and your experience

To remember preferences such as your music and sound settings

To understand which pages are useful and fix problems

To respond to your messages and moderate comments

To show advertising that helps cover the cost of the site

Cookies and advertising

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google’s advertising cookies allow it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or at aboutads.info. See our Cookie Policy for more.

Third-party services

We may use third-party services such as comment platforms (for example Disqus), analytics tools, font providers and advertising networks. These services may collect information under their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to read them.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run the site, when the law requires it or to protect our rights. We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, object to processing or withdraw consent. To make a request, email help@igi2.online and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Children’s privacy

IGI2.online is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy from time to time and will change the date above when we do. Questions? Email help@igi2.online.
